About Us

Hospitality Services is an event management and publishing company. It was founded in 1993 by Nouhad Dammous and his daughter Joumana.

The firm organizes trade shows, exhibitions, forums and virtual events that stand at the heart of the region’s food, hospitality and tourism industries. In addition, the company has decades of expertise in curating events for its clients, providing a customized service, from event design to production and logistics.

“By understanding the needs of our clients and what is happening in the market, we seek to serve our industry unique experiences that add value, encourage positive dialog and ultimately open the door to new business opportunities,” Joumana Dammous-Salamé, Managing Director.

With a talented team of over 20 professionals, Hospitality Services is constantly evolving, adapting to new ways of working in a digital era while finding smart and effective tools to connect hospitality and foodservice professionals.
